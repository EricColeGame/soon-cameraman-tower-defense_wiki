## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What makes a soon cameraman tower defense upgrade worth the investment?
A major upgrade tier is worth the price when it provides supplementary utility beyond raw damage—such as area-of-effect splash, armor penetration, or cooldown reductions. Upgrades that only offer small statistical increases should take a backseat to expanding your map coverage or investing in economy generators.

### Which targeting setting works best for upgraded Cameraman towers?
For single-target, high-damage units, set the targeting priority to **Strongest** or **Boss** so your highest damage is not wasted on low-health minions. For rapid-fire or splash-damage Cameraman units, leave the targeting on **First** to continually thin the front of incoming waves.

### How should beginners save resources for a soon cameraman tower defense upgrade?
Beginners should focus on deploying cost-effective early-game scouts that can clear the first five waves without upgrades. Use the breathing room to build economy towers, allowing you to save the lump sums required for mid- and late-game upgrade tiers without risking a premature loss.

### Can support units replace the need for higher damage tiers?
Support units multiply existing damage, but they cannot replace it entirely. A balanced squad utilizes support units to slow and debuff enemies while high-tier Cameraman units deliver the decisive finishing blows.
