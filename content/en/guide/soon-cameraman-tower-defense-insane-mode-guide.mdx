Do not overspend on early defenders. Drop a single cost-effective attacker at the first sharp bend of the map. Upgrade this unit only to the point where basic units cannot leak. Around wave 12, standard melee units are replaced by rapid clusters. Single-target units will become overwhelmed by target switching. Flying and shielded enemies enter the fray during this bracket. If your units lack "Camo" or "Air" targeting capabilities, your base will fall within seconds. Transition your focus away from farms and pour capital into leveling your primary mid-game carry. Upgrade your range perks to engage bosses before they enter their offensive stun radius.

Late-game waves spawn massive raid bosses with millions of hit points and sweeping disable abilities. Place your Titan units slightly set back from the track edge so frontal stuns do not immediately shut down your heaviest damage dealers. ## Advanced Micro-Management and Placement Tactics

Map geometry determines whether your towers hit their theoretical maximum DPS. Our **soon cameraman tower defense insane mode guide** focuses heavily on chokepoint management and manual target shifting rather than mindless spam.

### Maximizing Straight Lanes Over Corners

While hairpin turns are great for short-range splash towers, linear tracks are vastly superior for laser and railgun archetypes. Placing piercing units facing down a long straight segment ensures every shot penetrates multiple targets, effectively multiplying the unit's rated damage output.

### Targeting Priority Configurations

Leaving all towers on the default "First" targeting setting is a common mistake on Insane Mode. When a high-health tank walks ahead of fragile speedsters, your entire defensive line can get distracted by the tank while runners sprint past into your base.

- Set front-line heavy hitters to **Strongest** to chip down high-health tanks continuously.
- Set mid-lane splash units to **First** to clear out swift stragglers before they gain ground.
- Set rear defense towers to **Last** or **Closest** as a safety net against leaked threats.

### Mitigating Enemy Stun Attacks

Bosses in Insane Mode feature devastating shockwaves that disable towers for up to eight seconds. Counter this by creating a staggered defensive layout. Never group all your Titans in a single tight circle. By separating them into two or three distinct defensive pockets along the track, a stun directed at the vanguard leaves your secondary and tertiary units firing without interruption.
