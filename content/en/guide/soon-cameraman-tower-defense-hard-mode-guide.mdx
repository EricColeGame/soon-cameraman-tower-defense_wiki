### Targeting Protocol Adjustments

Leaving all towers set to default targeting ("First") is one of the quickest ways to fail high-tier runs:

1.  **Set Piercing/AoE to "First":** This ensures your splash attackers strip away fast-moving fodder units as soon as they round the corner.
2.  **Set Titan Finishers to "Strongest":** Boss-killing towers must ignore small swarms to continuously wear down the highest-health target on the field.
3.  **Set Support Slowers to "First":** Keeping the leading enemy slowed clumps following units closer together, amplifying the effectiveness of subsequent AoE attacks.

Find an S-curve, hairpin turn, or looping section of the track to serve as your primary killzone. Placing towers inside the inner curve maximizes their time-on-target compared to outer-edge placement. An inner-curve placement allows a camera unit to track targets along the approach, apex, and exit of the bend, effectively tripling its damage window.

Holding onto obsolete early-game units during the final waves is a common pitfall. Once wave 25 begins, basic vanguard units contribute negligible chip damage. If a boss bypasses your main defensive cluster, immediately sell out-of-range units and redeploy heavy hitters closer to your base exit for a final damage window.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is this soon cameraman tower defense hard mode guide focused so heavily on early-game economy units?
In Hard Mode, enemy health scaling rapidly outpaces passive round income. If you do not invest aggressively in economy generation within the first ten waves, you will not have sufficient cash reserves to purchase and fully upgrade high-tier boss-killer towers by wave 25.

### What is the biggest mistake players make during Hard Mode boss waves?
The most frequent mistake is leaving all units set to attack the "First" enemy. When bosses spawn alongside swarms of minions, your strongest towers waste their highest-damage projectiles on low-tier targets while the boss steadily marches toward the base. Always switch your primary boss-killers to "Strongest."

### How many slowing or crowd control towers should I deploy?
Two to three strategically positioned crowd control units are generally sufficient for most maps. Placing more than that leads to diminishing returns, taking up valuable track space that should be reserved for high-DPS piercing units and Titan finishers. Focus on positioning slow towers at major bends where their range rings cover multiple path segments.
