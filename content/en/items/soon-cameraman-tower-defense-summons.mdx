## Pro Summoning Tips to Beat RNG and Pity Systems

Gacha RNG can be unforgiving, but experienced players utilize proven methods to minimize risk. Applying smart summoning discipline transforms random rolls into predictable roster upgrades.

### 1. Track the Global Rotation Schedule
Developer cycles often telegraph future banner contents through seasonal themes or accompanying raid mechanics. When rumors circulate regarding **soon cameraman tower defense summons**, monitor community bulletin boards and announcement channels to confirm whether an upcoming banner shares pity with current banners.

### 2. Prioritize Multi-Summons Over Single Pulls
Almost all modern tower defense titles provide a subtle bonus for batch summons (such as a "10+1" bonus or discounted bundle costs). Performing single pulls wastes currency efficiency over time.

### 3. Identify Meta Traps
Not every newly released unit represents an automatic upgrade. Developers sometimes introduce high-rarity units that sport flashy visual effects but offer substandard damage-per-second-per-cost (DPS/Cost) ratios. Wait for day-one community testers to publish raw DPS benchmarks before draining your resources.
