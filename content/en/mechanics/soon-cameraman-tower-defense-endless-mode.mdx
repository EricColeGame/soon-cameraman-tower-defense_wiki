## Economy Scaling and Cash Flow Strategy

Cash generation governs success or failure in infinite modes. If your economy lags by even two waves, you will lack the funds required to drop your heavy hitters before armored bosses cross the threshold.

Many players make the mistake of over-defending between waves 1 and 15. Placing unnecessary defensive units drains capital that should otherwise be funneled directly into upgrading income generators. Managing your economy in the soon cameraman tower defense endless mode requires calculating break-even points: the exact number of waves needed for an income upgrade to pay for itself and yield pure profit.

```
Break-Even Waves = Upgrade Cost / Cash Generated Per Wave
```

If an upgrade costs $4,000 and generates $1,000 per wave, its break-even point is 4 waves. Upgrades with break-even points higher than 6 waves should generally be avoided once you pass wave 50, as you must pivot every credit into active damage output.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid in High-Wave Runs

1. **Clustering Units Too Close to Boss Paths:** Many late-game bosses feature area-of-effect stun stomps or rocket barrages that temporarily disable nearby defenders. Keep your support units spaced slightly outside the immediate roadway perimeter.
2. **Ignoring Target Priorities:** Leaving all towers set to "First" causes heavy boss melters to waste high-damage attacks on low-health minions. Always toggle your Titan Cameramen to "Strongest."
3. **Holding Unspent Cash in the Late Game:** After wave 65, cash sitting in your bank provides zero value. If your unit cap is full, sell lower-tier towers and replace them with higher-tier options, or continuously trigger activated unit skills.
4. **Neglecting Stun Decay:** Stun abilities suffer heavy diminishing returns on successive hits against the same enemy. Stagger your control units along the path rather than stacking them in one spot.
