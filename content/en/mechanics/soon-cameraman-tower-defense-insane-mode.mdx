## Step-by-Step Wave Progression Strategy

Pacing your spending determines whether you reach the endgame intact or fall to mid-wave runner swarms. To ensure your squad can overcome the soon cameraman tower defense insane mode difficulty, follow a methodical wave-by-wave strategy.

### Early Game: Waves 1 to 10
Your objective during the first ten waves is to secure base defense using the minimum viable investment while funneling excess cash into economic generators.

1. Deploy one or two low-cost frontline defenders directly at the first turn.
2. Upgrade your opening defender to level 2 to handle fast runners.
3. Place your first economy generator by Wave 3.
4. Upgrade your farms steadily, only pausing to add a secondary defender if mob clusters leak past the primary bend.

```
Wave 1-10 Economic Loop:
[Survive Wave] -> [Deposit Income into Farm] -> [Upgrade Farm Tier] -> [Hold Baseline Defense]
```

### Mid Game: Waves 11 to 25
The mid-game introduces armored units, fast mini-bosses, and split pathways. During this phase, relying strictly on single-target damage leads to defeat.

1. Establish your crowd-control zone. Place slowing or stun-capable units at the center of your primary kill zone.
2. Introduce splash-damage units to neutralize shielded packs before they stack up.
3. Finish leveling your economy units to maximum tier by Wave 20 to prepare for endgame costs.
4. Add secondary coverage slightly further down the path to pick off weakened stragglers.

### Late Game: Waves 26 to Final Boss
The final stretch tests your maximum sustained damage per second. Success requires selling low-impact early units and consolidating firepower into high-tier heavy hitters.

1. Begin phasing out baseline early-game units to free up strategic map slots.
2. Deploy your primary titan-class units within overlapping range of your crowd-control towers.
3. Prioritize upgrading titan damage and range over placing multiple low-level towers.
4. Keep a reserve cash pool to instantly replace or reposition units if targeted by boss debuffs.

## Optimal Positioning and Map Chokepoint Strategies

A unit's performance depends heavily on the geometry of its placement. A high-tier damage tower placed on a flat, straight line covers only a fraction of the distance it could cover at a loop or intersection.

Maximizing range overlap ensures your towers deliver continuous damage throughout an enemy's transit time. Placing your primary damage dealers inside track loops allows their attack radius to hit incoming targets, sweep across the loop, and strike exiting units from behind.

| Map Feature | Best Unit Archetype | Strategic Advantage | Common Placement Error |

Player experience consistently confirms that grouping your crowd-control support towers just ahead of your highest-damage titan units produces the best results. Slowing enemies inside your primary kill box multiplies the effective DPS of every unit targeting that sector.

### Over-Investing in Early Units
One common pitfall is placing too many low-tier units during early waves. While this stops initial leaks, low-tier units scale poorly and waste valuable board space. Invest in the absolute minimum defense needed to hold the line, then upgrade your income sources.

### Neglecting Crowd Control

### When should I stop upgrading my income units in insane mode?
